# MiniTask 1.1

This release brings a smaller toolbar and puts the most-used options in one place.

- Open, save, record, and play from a compact toolbar.
- Change speed, repeats, shortcuts, and appearance from **Prefs**.
- See recording time and playback progress at a glance.
- Hide button captions for an even smaller window.
- Open and save recordings with **Ctrl+O** and **Ctrl+S**.

Download **MiniTask-1.1-win-x64.zip**, extract it, and open **MiniTask.exe**. If you're updating, close the old copy first, including its tray icon. Your recordings and preferences are preserved.
