# MiniTask 1.1.2

A few small improvements to make MiniTask easier to use.

- A simpler shortcut menu with keys found on standard keyboards.
- Keys already assigned to another action are clearly marked.
- A consistent classic appearance across the toolbar, app windows, and system tray.

The default shortcuts are still **F8** to record, **F9** to play, and **F10** to stop.

Download **MiniTask-1.1.2-win-x64.zip**, extract it, and open **MiniTask.exe**. Close any older copy first, including its tray icon. Your recordings and preferences are preserved.
