# MiniTask 1.1.3

- Start a new recording with **F8** or **Rec** without being asked to save the previous one. Click **Save** beforehand to keep a recording.
- Optional sounds let you hear when recording or playback starts and stops. Enable **Prefs → Sound cues** to try them.

Download **MiniTask-1.1.3-win-x64.zip**, extract it, and open **MiniTask.exe**. Close any older copy first, including its tray icon. Saved recordings and preferences carry over.
